Read the comparison loosely. The two scans came from different machines (InBody270S vs the higher-resolution InBody570) with slightly different entered heights (5′8″ vs 170.2 cm), which shifts BMI and the "% of ideal" segmental targets — the drop in leg percentages is mostly that recalibration, since the leg mass itself barely moved. Hydration, time of day, and food before the test also nudge every number. For tracking, repeat tests on the same machine under similar conditions.
