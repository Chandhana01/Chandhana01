# Mechanical Engineering Bootcamps in America (2–3 Weeks)

A researched guide to short, intensive (roughly 2–3 week) mechanical engineering bootcamps and programs in the United States, compiled August 2026. Programs are grouped by audience, since "bootcamp" options differ a lot depending on whether you're a high school student, a college student, or a working professional.

## For High School Students

### 1. Engineering Summer Academy at Penn (ESAP) — Robotics
- **The closest true fit to "3-week mechanical engineering bootcamp."** Rigorous, college-level, residential program at the University of Pennsylvania.
- **2026 dates:** July 12–31 (3 weeks). **Tuition:** $9,250. College credit awarded.
- The **Robotics** track covers design, manufacturing, and automation — the most mechanical-engineering-flavored of ESAP's seven tracks (others include AI, Biotech, Nanotechnology).
- **Deadlines:** priority/international Jan 31; general Feb 28.
- Site: https://esap.seas.upenn.edu/

### 2. Columbia University SHAPE (Summer High School Academic Program for Engineers)
- Project-based, college-level engineering courses taught by Columbia faculty, including mechanical/robotics offerings (e.g., Robotics and Autonomous Driving).
- **Two 3-week sessions** each summer (historically early July and late July–mid August).
- Commuter and residential options in New York City.
- Site: https://outreach.engineering.columbia.edu/ (see Columbia SHAPE)

### 3. IEEE TryEngineering Summer Institute
- **9-day residential camps** run by IEEE at multiple campuses. Mechanical engineering modules cover mechanics of motion, structures, and the engineering design process, alongside exposure to other disciplines.
- **2026 sessions:** University of Pennsylvania (Jun 29–Jul 7, Jul 11–19), Rice University (Jun 29–Jul 7, Jul 11–19), University of San Diego (Jun 11–19, Jun 24–Jul 2), Columbia University (Jul 12–20, Jul 24–Aug 1).
- **Cost:** from $4,095; scholarships offered. Sessions sell out.
- Site: https://tryengineeringinstitute.ieee.org/

### 4. Manhattan University — Engineering Awareness Program
- **10-day intensive** for rising juniors/seniors, **free of charge**, geared toward women and underrepresented students in the NYC metro area. 2026 cohort: Aug 2–8.
- Site: https://manhattan.edu/academics/schools-and-departments/school-of-engineering/high-school-programs.php

### 5. Stevens Institute of Technology — Engineering Bootcamp
- Residential pre-college **crash course (~1 week)** with mechanical engineering modules; a good shorter alternative if 2–3 weeks doesn't fit.
- Site: https://www.stevens.edu/admission-aid/pre-college-programs/summer-residential-programs/engineering-bootcamp

*Adjacent options:* MIT's Women's Technology Program (4 weeks, mechanical engineering track, free, highly selective) and University of Maryland's ESTEEM/SER-Quest (4 weeks) are slightly longer but well regarded.

## For Working Professionals / College Students

True software-style "career-switch bootcamps" don't really exist for mechanical engineering (the field requires an accredited degree for most roles). What exists instead:

### MIT Professional Education — Short Programs
- 50+ intensive **2–5 day courses**, mostly June–August on the MIT campus (some live-virtual), in areas like mechatronics, design & manufacturing, modeling/simulation.
- **You can stack 2–4 courses back-to-back to build a custom 2–3 week bootcamp** in Cambridge, MA.
- **Cost:** roughly $900–$1,000/day (≈$1,050–$5,850 per course). Certificates awarded; many stack toward MIT Professional Certificate Programs.
- Catalog: https://professional.mit.edu/course-catalog

### ASME Learning & Development
- The professional society for mechanical engineers runs instructor-led, in-person courses (codes & standards — e.g., ASME Y14.5 GD&T, B31.3 piping, Section VIII pressure vessels) at rotating venues. 2026 examples: **Albuquerque May 4–8** and **Denver May 11–14**.
- Week-long blocks can similarly be combined across consecutive weeks.
- Catalog: https://www.asme.org/learning-development/find-course

### CAD / Design Skills Bootcamps
- Short intensives focused on employable tooling (SolidWorks, Fusion 360, 3D printing, prototyping) are offered by providers like FastBuildLabs (cove.design) and various NobleProg instructor-led courses; formats range from days to ~5 weeks, online and onsite. Verify current 2026 schedules directly.

---

## Practical Notes

- **The 2–3 week sweet spot is dominated by pre-college programs** (ESAP, SHAPE, TryEngineering). For adults, the realistic path is stacking 1-week professional short courses (MIT PE, ASME) or a focused CAD intensive.
- **Apply early:** flagship programs (ESAP, SHAPE) have Jan–Mar deadlines and the IEEE camps sell out.
- **Budget range:** free (Manhattan University, MIT WTP) → ~$4–6k (IEEE, SHAPE) → ~$9k+ (ESAP); professional courses run ~$1k/day.
- **Financial aid/scholarships** are available at most of the paid pre-college programs.
