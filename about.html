I’m Chandhana — a self-taught artist, writer, and stand-up comic. I grew up in Chennai, and I now work with elements at UNC.

Some things I’ve done along the way: set a Guinness record during the pandemic, done competitive stand-up comedy, made a baby rocket and a baby dagger, and had high tea at the House of Lords.

This site is a small map of the things I make — writing and art.
