About

Screw the moon, I’m going to Mars. 🌑

I’m Chandhana — a researcher, writer, and artist working toward a future that is healthier and more sustainable.

I’ve made edible bioplastics from seaweed for less than a dollar, researched brains and brain-computer interfaces as the youngest intern at IIT Madras’s neurotech lab, published a book about neuroscience, set a Guinness World Record, and won a climate tech contest in Italy. I’ve worked alongside Walmart to design the Store of the Future, and I’m an Emergent Ventures grantee.

When I’m not in a lab or behind a keyboard, I’m probably pointing a camera at the night sky, learning to figure skate, or doing stand-up comedy.

This site is a small map of the things I make and think about — writing, art, and whatever comes next.
